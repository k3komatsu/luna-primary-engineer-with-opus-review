Maintain this conceptually in the Primary session or a small local note if needed.

```text

Last-use recency: recent | stale | unknown
Context health: clean | growing | polluted
```

## Opus

Panel members start fresh. Promote only a genuinely useful member to sticky advisor. Resume it for same-domain follow-ups with small deltas.

Reviewer sessions are **not advisor sessions**. Never reuse an advisor that helped design the solution as the supposedly independent first reviewer of that solution.

## Sol / Astra

Reuse when scope and assumptions substantially overlap. Prefer a delta follow-up to re-sending a large stable prefix.

## Spawn fresh when

- scope materially changes;
- assumptions changed;
- independence is the point;
- stale branches bias the answer;
- context pollution outweighs continuity.

## Compaction

When a sticky expert becomes bloated, distill:

```markdown
# Expert State
## Scope
## Stable invariants
## Accepted decisions
## Rejected alternatives and reasons
## Critical evidence
## Open questions
## Current assumptions
```

Then seed a fresh generation rather than dragging unrelated history forever.
